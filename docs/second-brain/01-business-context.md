# Business Context

The dashboard exists to help the company understand whether Sales is on track and whether Won business turns into recognizable revenue and invoice progress.

## Primary Audiences

- Sales management: target achievement, pipeline risk, sale owner performance.
- Sale owners: counted deals, open opportunities, missing data, lead creation.
- Finance: recurring revenue schedule, revenue distribution, performance against recurring target.
- Accounting: invoice analysis, service period view, revenue-vs-invoice reconciliation.
- Executives: summary performance, target achievement, revenue trend, data quality.

## Main Business Questions

- Which sale owners and groups are achieving target?
- Which Won deals count toward company sales performance?
- Which open deals create pipeline risk?
- Which revenue will be recognized by month?
- How does recognized recurring revenue compare with the recurring target?
- How does recognized revenue compare with invoice progress?
- Are new leads being created consistently by week?
- Which data issues reduce dashboard trust?

## Important Vocabulary

- Deal: CRM opportunity.
- Counted Deal: deal eligible for sales performance because pipeline matching says it should count.
- All Deals: audit view for included, excluded, and unmapped deals.
- Transaction Detail: counted deal details behind Sales Performance.
- Sale Type: New or Renew sales motion.
- Billing Type: One-time/OTC or Recurring revenue behavior.
- Won: successful deal. Pre-WON is treated as Won.
- Lost: unsuccessful deal. Pre-LOST is treated as Lost.
- Revenue Schedule: monthly recognition plan from Won / Pre-WON deals.
- Recurring Target: planning baseline from previous-year recurring revenue.
- Invoice Analysis: invoice amount distribution by Posting Date or Service Period.
- Revenue Invoice Reconciliation: customer-level comparison of recognized revenue and invoice amount.

## Concept Boundaries

- New/Renew is a sales classification.
- One-time/Recurring is a billing classification.
- Sales Performance is not Revenue Performance.
- Expected Close Date is a sales tracking date.
- Contract Start Date is the preferred revenue recognition start date.
- Created Date is the New Deals lead creation date.
