# Dashboard Tabs

This document describes what each tab means and what it should not be confused with.

## Overview

Purpose:

- Executive summary of sales achievement and pipeline status.
- Shows target achievement, status mix, and top open opportunities.

Use for:

- Quick management review.
- Spotting high-value opportunities and high-level performance.

Do not use for:

- Finance reconciliation.
- Full deal audit.

## Sales Performance

Purpose:

- Show counted deals and achievement against Sale Target.
- Show Renew, New, Total, and cumulative charts.

Important behavior:

- Uses counted pipeline scope.
- Uses Expected Close Date for sales period tracking.
- Default chart status filter shows Won.

Do not confuse with:

- Revenue Analysis, which uses contract/service timing.

## Revenue Analysis

Purpose:

- Finance-oriented view of revenue schedules, recurring targets, invoice analysis, and reconciliation.

Includes:

- Revenue Performance
- Revenue Analysis
- Recurring Target from Last Year Revenue
- Revenue Distribution
- Invoice Analysis
- Revenue Detail
- Target Contribution Detail
- Revenue Data Checks

Use for:

- Monthly revenue planning.
- Finance and accounting review.
- Reconciliation between recognized revenue and invoice progress.

## All Deals

Purpose:

- Audit all selected deals, including included, excluded, and unmapped deals.

Use for:

- Explaining why a deal is not counted.
- Reviewing raw deal rows against mapping and pipeline matching.

Do not use for:

- Sales achievement totals without checking counting status.

## Pipeline Risk

Purpose:

- Show open counted deals with execution risk.

Risk signals include:

- overdue expected close date
- missing expected date
- stale stage
- not contacted

Do not use for:

- Data quality mapping errors.
- Revenue risk.

## Data Quality

Purpose:

- Show missing fields, mapping health, unmapped names, and assumptions.

Use for:

- Improving trust in dashboard totals.
- Identifying required source data fixes.

## New Deals

Purpose:

- Measure lead/deal creation activity by ISO Week.

Important behavior:

- Uses Created Date.
- Uses its own ISO Week filter.
- It is not a revenue or sales achievement view.

Do not confuse:

- New Deals tab with New sale type.

## Setting

Purpose:

- Upload CSV files.
- Refresh dashboard.
- Edit Pipeline Matching.
- Load mapping/target setup.

Use carefully because mappings affect counted sales totals.
